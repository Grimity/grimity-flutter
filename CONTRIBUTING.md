# Contributing

Submit patches or pull requests through Grimity's designated contribution channels. Contributions must comply with the [Grimity Source-Available License v1.0](LICENSE).

## AI-assisted contributions

AI tools may assist contributors, subject to the AI/ML restrictions in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Source code, prompts containing it, and outputs derived from it must not be used or made available for model training, model improvement, or construction of prohibited AI/ML datasets.

Contributions must include substantive human authorship or substantive human revision of the implementation. Contributions whose implementation is produced entirely by AI, without such human authorship or revision, are prohibited.

Prompting an AI tool, copying its output, formatting it, or reviewing or testing it without substantive human authorship or revision is not sufficient to satisfy this requirement.

Contributors must understand, review, and verify the code they submit and remain responsible for its correctness and their right to submit it.

These contribution guidelines do not grant additional rights to use, redistribute, or commercially exploit the Software beyond those granted by LICENSE.
